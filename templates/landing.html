How It Works

From Raw Data to
Smart Prediction

A four-step automated pipeline turns Yahoo Finance data into actionable insights.

1
Data Collection
Fetches 2 years of OHLCV data for 8 NSE stocks via yFinance. Computes MA20, MA50, RSI, MACD and stores in SQLite.
2
Graph Construction
Calculates Pearson correlations between all stock pairs. Draws edges where correlation > 0.4, then runs graph algorithms.
3
Model Training
Trains separate XGBoost and RandomForest models per stock using engineered technical indicator features with 80/20 train-test split.
4
Prediction & Analysis
Loads saved models to predict next-day price and direction. Combines with probability scores and graph centrality for a complete signal.
